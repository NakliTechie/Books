# Lorewell brand

Lorewell is a private semantic library for the books you own.

**Product line:** Your private semantic library.

**Launch line:** Draw more from what you read.

## Mark

The Living Book mark combines three ideas:

- an open book for faithful reading;
- a subtle `B` formed by the right-hand page for continuity with the app's
  stable `books` identity; and
- one burnt-sienna node connected to the spine for ideas that become part of
  a living, cross-book library.

Use `logo-lockup.svg` when the product name has room. Use `logo-mark.svg` on
the Lorewell cream surface, and `favicon.svg` where the mark needs its own
background. Do not recolour, rotate, outline, or add more concept nodes.

## Product identity and compatibility

The visible product name and canonical hostname are **Lorewell** and
`lorewell.naklitechie.com`. Internal app IDs, storage paths, schemas, and the
GitHub repository retain `books` where changing them would break existing
libraries or NakliOS integration. `books.naklitechie.com` is a permanent
redirect, not a second product identity.
