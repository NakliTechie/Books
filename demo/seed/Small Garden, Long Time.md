# Small Garden, Long Time

The gardener did not begin with a picture of the finished tree. She began by
learning which branch had become possible.

Each season left evidence: a scar where wire had rested, a new angle toward
light, a root that made the shallow pot feel suddenly generous. Pruning was
not subtraction for its own sake. It was a decision about where the living
story could continue.

Years later, visitors called the bonsai timeless. The gardener smiled. What
they were seeing was time made visible.
